# Omar Interview Preparation

Reference materials for the current TransPerfect Gaming and TapTap Send interview processes.

The source PDFs are stored alongside this index. The materials cover interview answers, behavioral questions, French call simulations, gaming-support roleplays, escalation logic, and interview-day checklists.

> Note: TransPerfect assessment questions/material are not reproduced here. Omar's preparation documents explicitly state not to disclose assessment content because of the signed Applicant Testing Agreement.

## Files

- `Omar-Interview-Scenario-and-Call-Simulation-Playbook.pdf` — intensive interview and call-simulation playbook for TransPerfect Gaming and TapTap Send.
- `Omar-Interview-Cheat-Sheet-or-TransPerfect-Gaming-TapTap-Send.pdf` — primary interview field manual / cheat sheet.
- `Omar-Interview-Cheat-Sheet-or-TransPerfect-Gaming-TapTap-Send (2).pdf` — duplicate/alternate copy of the cheat sheet.

## Core preparation framework

The documents emphasize a support flow of opening, acknowledgement, ownership, probing, verification, and resolution/closure. A recurring rule is to verify before promising a solution, and to be transparent when information is unknown.

The materials are based on Omar's real experience and researched role information; they explicitly instruct against inventing policies, refunds, timelines, tools, metrics, or achievements.
